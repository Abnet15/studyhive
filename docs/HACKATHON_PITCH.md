# The BiT AI Hackathon Pitch 🎤

## Title: StudyHive - The AI-Powered Learning Hub
**Track:** Edu-Tech
**Spotlight Target:** Askuala Link 

---

### The Hook (0:00 - 0:30)
"Good afternoon judges. How many times have you been handed a 50-page PDF course syllabus or lecture note, only to spend hours trying to figure out what the actual core concepts were? What if the material could *speak* to you? Enter **StudyHive**."

### The Problem (0:30 - 1:00)
"Current educational platforms are static dumpsters. Students upload files, and they sit there gathering digital dust. Askuala Link asked for the *next big thing in education*. The next big thing isn't just hosting files; it's **understanding them**."

### The Solution (1:00 - 2:00)
"StudyHive is an intelligent, AI-first knowledge repository. We migrated our entire architecture to a MongoDB NoSQL infrastructure connected directly to **Cloudinary's global CDN**. This means zero lag and infinite scalability. But here is the magic..."

*(Show Demo of uploading a material)*

"When a student uploads a complex PDF, our backend doesn't just save it. It intercepts the file stream, pipes it through the Askuala AI Engine, powered by Google's cutting edge Gemini 3.1 Pro model. It instantly reads the entire document, generates a Smart Summary, extracts Key Terms, and even generates a self-test Quiz—all automatically, in seconds."

### The Architecture & Reliability (2:00 - 2:30)
"To prove this is production-ready, we built a highly robust fallback AI pipeline. If our primary AI model is congested, our system automatically falls back step-by-step through Google's other models (like Gemini 2.5 Flash) ensuring a 100% success rate. We even integrated automated CI/CD deployment pipelines."

### The Askuala Link Pitch (2:30 - 3:00)
"StudyHive aligns perfectly with Askuala Link's vision. By replacing tedious studying with AI-accelerated automated learning paths, we are giving every single student a personalized AI Tutor. We are ready to partner, scale, and transform Ethiopian education."

"Build the Future. Ship the Future. StudyHive."
